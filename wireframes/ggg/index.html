— A Note from the Bench
“I mill from felled-on-site timber because it's the only way to know what a tree was doing before it became a table.”

The slab on the Lower Hollow Table came from a black walnut that was already a century old when Josh and Abigail bought this land in 2018. The 2022 derecho took it down. We milled it on a portable bandsaw in the barn that week and air-dried it under cover until last spring.

That's a hundred-and-eight-year story attached to one piece of furniture, and I think you can feel it when you sit down at the table.

— George · The Workshop
