The drying schedule for a 4″ walnut slab

The rule of thumb says one year per inch of thickness. That's a starting point, not a finish line. A 4-inch walnut slab takes us closer to six years from felling to bench-ready — and there are things you do in years three and four that determine whether the slab cups or stays flat. Stickers, weight, end-grain sealer, and the importance of an actually-dry shed.

Method · Mar 28, 2026 · 12-min read

Why we mill from felled-on-site timber

The economics of buying lumber are better. The wood is more uniform. The lead time is shorter. We mill from on-site felled trees anyway, because the alternative is anonymity — and a piece of furniture with an anonymous origin can't tell you any of the things that the Lower Hollow Table can tell you about a First-gen windstorm and a grandfather who planted the wrong tree in the right place.
