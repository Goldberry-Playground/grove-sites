— Notes from the propagation bed

Why M.111

M.111 gives Honeycrisp the cold-hardiness it needs in Zone 6. Dwarf rootstocks (B.9, M.27) don't survive our winters. M.111 makes a 14–18 ft tree, which is plenty productive without ladder work.

Bitter pit

Honeycrisp is famously susceptible to bitter pit — a calcium deficiency that shows as small brown spots in the flesh. Soil test for calcium. Don't push nitrogen. Drip irrigation, not flood. The first three years matter most.

Storage

The variety's killer feature: it stores 5–7 months at 32°F without losing crispness. A root cellar at 34–38°F gets you four. Outside a cellar, eat it within two months of harvest — flavor flattens out fast in warm storage.
