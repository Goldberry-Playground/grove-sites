Choosing rootstock for Zones 5 and 6 — the short list

Most rootstock charts are written for the mid-Atlantic. If you're north of Zone 5, the conversation gets shorter, the choices get fewer, and the trade-offs get sharper. M.111 stays — it's our default. B.9 if you need full dwarf. EMLA 7 if you want semi-dwarf with full hardiness. Almost everything else fails before it sets fruit.
