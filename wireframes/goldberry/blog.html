Method · March 2, 2026

What we learned overwintering garlic under a foot of straw

The cloves we planted in October pushed through the snow line in late February — every bed except the one we forgot to mulch. Lessons: straw is non-optional, the variety matters more than the calendar, and the deer will, in fact, take the time to push aside the straw if they're hungry enough.
